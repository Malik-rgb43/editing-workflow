# Words to delete, words to write

Load when: a draft reads generic, or a result looks "AI". Dated 2026-10-03. Most of the AI look is removed in the edit and the grade (one look, grain, native fps, real sound), not by prompt words; the prompt's job is to be specific.

## 1. Delete, then replace
| Delete (nothing measures it) | Write instead |
|---|---|
| cinematic, epic, stunning, beautiful, amazing, masterpiece, breathtaking | a lens ("35 mm"), a light direction ("low sun from camera left"), a movement or a number |
| 8K, ultra-realistic, award-winning, trending | a stock or format ("35 mm film", "phone night mode") |
| photorealistic (as a charm) | "natural skin texture, subtle imperfections", pore-level detail only when the shot is a close-up |
| a sad man | "jaw tight, eyes narrowed, shoulders dropped" (acting in muscles) |
| a woman is in an alley | "a woman strides through a rain-soaked alley, coat flapping, boots splashing" (observable physics) |
| modern office | "glass wall, grey carpet, one desk lamp on, blinds half down" (materials and one light) |

## 2. Skin, light, lens, materials
- **Skin:** natural texture, vellus hair, asymmetric details; avoid "flawless" and "airbrushed".
- **Light:** one motivated source; say which side; haze or dust in the beam if it exists; shadow-side camera for drama; no "perfect even light" unless it is a sheet.
- **Lens and stock:** name one; neutral stocks (16 mm, 35 mm 500T) read as film; avoid mixing two looks in one prefix.
- **Materials:** scratches, dust, patina, wet reflections; period by materials and light, not by the word "vintage".
- **Hands, text, props:** design the frame so they are not the point; models drift on them across cuts.

## 3. Positive constraints
`locked-off static camera` not "no shaky camera"; `sharp focus throughout, deep depth of field` not "no blur"; `anatomically correct, all limbs naturally positioned` not "no extra limbs". The only negatives kept: `no text, no labels, no watermark`.

## 4. Safety filters
Describe the scene as a filmmaker would (setting, camera, light, physics), not a subject as a note to a friend. Real names and age words are replaced by archetype and visual description; a brand is replaced by a clean plate (a blank screen or sign) and the real asset is composited in post. Do not loop on single-word swaps.
