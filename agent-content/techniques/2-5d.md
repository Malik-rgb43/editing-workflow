## 0. When to use it

2.5D turns **one still** into a moving shot by splitting it into depth layers and moving the layers at different speeds (a camera move with parallax). It is the owner's fallback whenever a local or paid image-to-video shot is too slow or too risky.

- **The 30-minute gate `[RULE-owner]` `[PROVEN-internal]`:** benchmark **one second** of the generative route first; if the ETA for one shot is **> 30 min**, propose 2.5D (or another alternative) *immediately*, before starting the long job. Historical trigger: local Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B on the one reference machine needed ~17 min for 2 s at 544 px and ~1 h for 4–5 s; about 2 h of agent time was spent and the owner said "find an alternative". (src: distilled/03 §1, 2026-10-02)
- **What it can do:** push-in, pull-back, slow lateral move, rack-like depth separation, a light beam passing; people cut out on their own layer with a small breathing scale; live video inside screens.
- **What it cannot do `[MEASURED-lab]`:** invent new actions, articulation (a head turning, a hand moving) or a new viewpoint. If the brief needs those, reframe the beat with the person or queue a costed paid route behind the 💲 gate (`paid-generation-gate`). Owner rule for people: **AI-looking stills of people are never used as B-roll** ("looks AI"); use real footage, a matte cutout of the real speaker, a Blender render or a rebuilt UI. 2.5D is for plates, objects and real photographs/cutouts.

## 1. Measured numbers (E10, `[MEASURED-lab]`, OM, single passes on a shared host)

| Route | Setup | Result | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local generative video** — Wan 2.1 T2V 1.3B fp16 + Q4 text encoder via stable-diffusion.cpp (Vulkan, CPU offload) | 512×512, 33 frames at 16 fps (2.06 s), 20 steps, cfg 6, seed 42 | **wall 1,380 s ≈ 669 s per output second (≈ 11 min per second of video)**; peak RSS 18.4 GB; split: text encode 119 s, weight load 45 s, sampling 446 s (~22 s/step), CPU VAE decode 804 s. GPU VAE crashed twice (0xC0000409, also with tiling). Smoke test 256², 9 frames, 2 steps = 261 s | one resolution/length; coherent rocking boat with shape wobble in 2 of 9 sampled frames (by eye, **no human rating**); LTX, 480p+, ComfyUI/ROCm not run |
| **2.5D** — Depth Anything V2 **Small** (CPU torch, 8 threads) → 3 layers → affine camera | 512 px, 49 frames rendered / 48 encoded at 24 fps = 2 s | **8.1–9.9 s total** (two stills: 8.08 s and 9.73 s); depth ≈ 0.9 s warm, model load ≈ 0.8 s, layer build + inpaint ≈ 1.1 s, frame render 2.2–3.0 s, encode ≈ 0.8 s; peak RSS ≈ 650 MB | artifacts: ghost edges and seam lines where quantile layers cut through a person; newly exposed (inpainted) area 1.3–2.3 % of the frame at mid-move |
| 2.5D layers composited in **HyperFrames** | same layers, 1080² | **13.3 s for 48 frames** | same artifacts |

**Gate (E10):** for plain camera moves on stills 2.5D is **~80× cheaper** than local Wan; these are *different tasks* and *not equal quality* — do not quote it as a speedup of the same output. Use local Wan only for non-urgent 2 s inserts and only after the GPU-VAE crash is fixed. Treat the layering artifacts as the quality limit. (src: E10 REPORT, results/parallax-*-metrics.json)

## 3. Recipe (the route E10 measured; original implementation, not a vendor skill)

1. **Prepare the still:** square or target aspect; for 9:16 work at 1080×1920 for the final composite, at 512 px only for the quick test. Record the source and licence.
2. **Depth:** run Depth Anything V2 Small → relative inverse depth map (1 = near). Normalise to 0–1, blur σ ≈ 1.5 px.
3. **Layers:** split into **3 layers by depth quantile** (E10 used the 45th and 78th percentile of the blurred depth → pixel fractions ≈ 45 / 33 / 22 %). Far layer opaque; nearer layers get a soft alpha (σ ≈ 1.2 px).
4. **Occlusion fill:** for each layer, inpaint the area covered by nearer layers (dilate the hole 9×9, Telea inpaint, one-time) so the layer has content behind the nearer ones.
5. **Camera:** an **affine** move with a per-layer parallax weight (E10: far 0.15 / mid 0.55 / near 1.0), a sinusoidal or eased lateral amplitude of ≈ 10 px at 512 px (the design spec capped translation ≤ 8 px; E10 used 10), and a mild zoom (≈ +4 % over the shot). Rule from the owner's work: the **layer scale ≤ 1.2 % anchored at the contact point** when a person layer sits on a plate, or the plate's outline shows.
6. **Compositing in HyperFrames:** export the layers as PNG with alpha and animate them as transforms on one paused timeline (seek-safe). A light beam goes in **screen blend UNDER the person layer** — a beam above the person whitened him and left a double outline (a 7.4 min re-render). Live video inside screens is its own layer (media is composited in its own layer).
7. **Acceptance review at 1× speed and at a paused frame:** check (a) ghost edges / seam lines on any person or on fine structure, (b) holes (exposed inpaint) in the first and last 10 frames, (c) the move is one continuous spline (no stall), (d) the shot still reads in the feed (text ≥ 32 px). Record the result per shot in `hf/QA.md`.

Planned tool: `depth_25d` (TOOLS_SPEC §3 item 10, P2 — **not yet implemented**); until it exists the recipe above is the spec. Output of the tool must carry the QA envelope fields (input hash, model id + licence, frames, status, findings) and a timing-ledger line.

## 4. Decision table (per shot)

| Condition | Route |
|---|---|
| plain camera move on a still is enough | **2.5D** |
| needs a person to move/articulate or a new viewpoint | reframe the beat, or a 💲 paid image-to-video route with a retry cap, or real footage |
| a clean 1.2–2.5 s window of a generation is enough | a short generation; benchmark 1 s first |
| ETA of the generative route > 30 min per shot | **2.5D immediately** |
| photoreal hero object, no footage | Blender turntable (see the 3D decision in the type skills) |
| procedural / data-driven / many instances | Three.js route |

## 5. Failure modes

| Symptom | Cause | Remedy | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| ghost edge / seam on a person | quantile layers cut through the subject | cut the person out as their own layer (matte) and parallax the plate behind | acceptance review (§3.7); matte only the beats that need it |
| revealed holes look smeared | large amplitude, thin inpaint | reduce amplitude, widen the fill, shorter move | keep exposed area ≈ ≤ 2.3 % (the measured band — a measurement, not a standard) |
| plate outline visible around a person | layer scale or beam above the person | beam under the person, scale ≤ 1.2 % anchored at the contact point | §3.5–3.6 |
| the shot "looks like a slideshow" | no secondary motion or event | add a light beam, breathing scale, live video in a screen, a pulse | clean-and-smooth D1 |
| used Base/Large weights in a client project | licence not read | stop, remove, re-run with Small | §2 |
| quoted "80× faster" to a client | different tasks, unequal quality | quote both numbers with their limits | §1 gate wording |
