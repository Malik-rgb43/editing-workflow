## 3. What is measured

| Outcome (suite spec) | Deterministic check | Human check |
|---|---|---|
| product integrity | silhouette/colour of the product frames vs `product_spec.json` (shape ratio, hue); no stretching, no recolour outside the three declared colourways | reviewer compares frames to the stills |
| factual copy | every price/date/claim on screen equals `copy.<lang>.json` (digits exact; ₪ placement as written; Hebrew spelling) — a changed number is a **severe failure** | Hebrew/English reader |
| UI safety | key text box ⊂ safe rows; the caption rail ≤ y 1450; collision solver on declared boxes | overlay snapshots at hook, offer, end card |
| final CTA hold and readability | CTA on screen ≥ 6 s (house preset), contrast ≥ 4.5:1 sampled, end card 2–3 s | phone-scale look |
| sound synchronisation | SFX within ±3 frames of the visible event (price reveal, logo hit); music licence rows present; −14 ± 0.5 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 on the final file; one loudness peak on the logo/CTA | listening, mute test |
| pace (informational) | cuts/min and median shot from frames vs `bands.json` ad-promo (descriptive, not a score) | — |

**Hard gates (from the rubric):** A1 offer ×3 and CTA; A2 brand ≤ 1 s and product ≤ 3 s; A6 licensed sound and level; delivery gate. Any claim not in `copy.<lang>.json` is a severe failure.

A synthetic bed cannot test licence handling of real music (that is covered by the licence-row check, not by audio quality).
