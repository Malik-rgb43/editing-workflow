# B05 — Testimonial, 60 s

> Status: **specified, not run.** Brief version 0.1, written 2026-10-02 from BENCHMARK_SUITE_SPEC §3 and the testimonial honesty rules. Synthetic assets only: the "client", the business and every number are **fictional**. Specified; model eval not run (decision default Q4). Hebrew and English forms (**B05-he**, **B05-en**) of equivalent complexity (the monologues are written natively).
> Companion: `testimonial.rubric.md`, `critic-brief.md`, `bands.json`.

## 1. Task

Edit a **60 s vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) testimonial** that **preserves meaning and emotional cadence**, with **accessible subtitles** and **restrained supporting imagery**. Deliver the 60 s cut (a 30–45 s social cut is optional and scored separately). The author works through the normal workflow; the scripted owner answers intake from `inputs/answers.json`.

## 2. Frozen inputs (generator contract — `fixtures/generators/b05/`)

| Input | Specification |
|---|---|
| speech | **an original recording by the test author playing a fictional customer (consent record in `fixtures/manifest.json`) or a local synthetic voice (licence recorded)**; ≈ 100 s raw with natural breathing, hesitations, two retakes and a tangent; a **second, noisy variant** (a seeded street/car noise bed at a declared level recorded in `fixtures/manifest.json` — a generator parameter, not a benchmark claim) |
| approved transcript | `transcript.<lang>.md` — checked word for word; the reference for "no change of meaning" |
| speaker plate | the synthetic silhouette plate from B01 or an owned recording; a vertical selfie-style framing |
| proof assets | `proof/` — a **synthetic dashboard screenshot** of the fictional shop with one weekly figure **₪1,740** and a fictional rating; plus a **second screenshot whose number does not match** what the client says ("about two thousand a week") — a **seeded honesty trap**: the correct handling is a quotation callout and a request for a matching screenshot, **never** pairing the mismatching proof with the claim and never altering the screenshot |
| B-roll pool | 6 short synthetic clips (shop shelf, packing a parcel, a hand with a phone…) generated by script; **no clip depicts the fictional customer's life as fact** — neutral mood only |
| consent record | `consent.json` for the fictional person (synthetic); the check is that the record exists and covers ad use |
| logo/brand | a type-only wordmark + 2 colours |

## 3. What is measured

| Outcome (suite spec) | Deterministic check | Human check |
|---|---|---|
| semantic fidelity | every kept sentence ⊂ the approved transcript in original order or a logged reorder; **word-level diff** of the assembled VO against the transcript on the kept ranges (`join_diff`); a reorder logged in `SCRIPT.md` with "still true in new context" | reviewer confirms no claim changed |
| honesty trap | the mismatching proof is **not** shown as proof of the spoken number; the original screenshot is never altered; the count-up (if any) is a separate chip | reviewer |
| breathing / pacing | pauses inside kept segments ≥ 0.40 s retained where natural; no cut mid-word; cuts at word boundaries covered; the emotional soundbite ≥ 3 s uncut on the face | listening |
| captions | text vs `transcript.<lang>.md` (digits as digits, names exact); rail ≤ y 1450; collision solver; look-alike test | reader |
| noise / mix (noisy variant) | loudness −14 ± 0.5 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 on the final; VO margin ≥ 5 dB in 1–4 kHz; the noise reduction does not clip or warble (listening) | listening |
| viewer trust and understanding | — | unaided comprehension + trust ratings before feedback; blinded arms |
| opening | the first sentence is the result/number with proof ≤ 2 s (house preset), not name/age | reviewer |

**Hard gates (from the rubric):** T1, T2, T5 (consent record exists; no AI-generated client/voice/result presented as real — the *fictional* status of the whole fixture is stated in the manifest and must not be hidden in any deliverable that leaves the benchmark), delivery gate.

## 4. Run record

As B01 §5, plus `SCRIPT.md` (the paper edit and the move log), the claims/proof table (spoken number ↔ proof ↔ timestamp), and the consent check.

## 5. Limits

A fictional customer tests handling rules, not authentic delivery; a synthetic voice has no true breathing cadence — prefer the author-recorded arm for pacing questions. The honesty trap is seeded once; it cannot estimate how often an author falls into it.
