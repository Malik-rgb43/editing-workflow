# Blueprint, hooks, visual grammar, rubric and numbers for testimonials

Load when: writing the paper edit, choosing hooks, designing proof layouts, or scoring a draft. Sources: distilled 02 video-types §4 and qa-and-benchmarks §6.2 (2026-10-01). Medians are the owner's analysis of 10 market references (9 of them 16:9) and 10 of his own selfie testimonials: small samples, different formats, not benchmarks.

## 1. Blueprint by length (seconds in the cut)
| Beat | 30-45 s | 60-90 s | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | 0-3 | the killer number or moment in the customer's words, proof on screen by about 2 s; lower-third at 1-4 s |
| Before / pain | 3-10 | 3-20 | the pain with a number ("six weeks"); music out for the crisis (optional black and white) |
| Turning point | 10-15 | 20-35 | the mentor or product enters only AFTER the problem; in the long cut, kill an objection |
| Result / proof | 15-32 | 35-65 | stepped numbers (for example 40K, then 100K, then 200K); repeat the hook number at the result |
| Emotional close | 32-40 | 65-82 | the strongest emotional line, 3-4 s uncut on the face |
| CTA | 40-45 | 82-90 | in the customer's words + end card <= 3 s with logo and prompt |
The killer NUMBER goes in the hook and again at the result; the killer EMOTION goes at the close. A hook line over 3 s is acceptable up to about 4.5 s if the number lands by 3 s and is on screen from frame 0. Repeating the hook number at the result is visual only (callout or headline); replay audio only if it is a different take. Versions from one interview: ad 15-30 s, social 45-60 s, full 90 s or more, plus an optional multi-client supercut. Default ratio 9:16 (`multi-video-variants` for others).

## 2. Hooks, ranked (and the openers to avoid)
1. **Result + proof** - "last month I closed over 500K in the store"; "I bought a bag and within a week I passed it".
2. **Twist** - "I offered him 150K for coaching, he said no".
3. **Pain with a number** - "course after course... I fell for it".
4. **Authority superlative** - "if there is one person I would learn from... without a doubt".
5. **Supercut** - 3 clients, 3 numbers, 3 s.
Forbidden openers: name and age; "hi, what's up" / "okay so I"; "I want to recommend"; starting mid-sentence; a logo or title first. The owner's pattern in 4 of 5 analysed testimonials: the proof arrived 4-46 s after a name-and-age opening, or never; in the market the result arrives at 0-3 s. No source verifies a universal "3-second rule" for platforms: this is editorial practice for trust, not ranking law.
Never invent a number: if no sentence has a specific, open on a pain or a twist and say why.

## 3. Visual grammar
- **Punch-ins:** alternate 100 % and 110-120 % on jump cuts, eye-line held. Speaker full-frame; the host appears as 0.5-1 s reaction cutaways; questions as titles; an equal 3-way Zoom stack is a weak layout.
- **Split-screen proof (signature, upgraded):** the original screenshot in the top 40 %, the speaker below, caption on the seam; hard cut on the frame of the number word; hold 3-6 s; a red marker ellipse on the figure; push-in 105 to 115 %, dim to the face's luma. The screenshot is never animated or rebuilt: only the chip beside it counts up (about 0.8 s).
- **Result block:** about 1:1 talk to proof; the client's own material first; B-roll only of the words said (life after, product in use); ask the client before using stock, and never let stock imply the client's life.
- **Lower-third** once at 1-4 s: "Name, age | role"; add the credential for authority endorsements. An optional persistent result headline sits in the top band for muted viewers and hides while a split-screen is up; during a hook split the lower-third sits at about 78 % and the caption stays on the seam.
- **End:** end card <= 3 s with logo and the CTA in the customer's words; no trailing black (references wasted 5-9 s on it). Long thank-yous become one line.
- **Sources of weakness to fix, not hide:** night or car footage (colour correction), noisy street/car audio (voice separation), driving footage (flag and request a reshoot), long static shots (a punch-in, proof or B-roll every <= 3 s).

## 4. Numbers (medians; small samples; check the unit before quoting)
| Metric | Market (10 refs, 9 x 16:9) | Owner's own selfie testimonials (9:16) | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| duration s | 47.0 | 54.6 | down toward 30-45 / 60-90 |
| cuts/min | 19.7 | 6.75 | up |
| median shot s | 2.37 | 4.85 | down |
| visual events/min | 22.6 | 6.7 | up |
| music ratio | 0.59 | 0.20 | up (a soft bed) |
| LUFS | -14.15 | -18.6 | to -14 |
Winning market format: 28-37 s of content, 23-36 cuts/min, shots 1.6-2.6 s; the weakest references were the slowest. Whether the slow selfie pace is intentional authenticity or a gap is the owner's open question: keep authenticity (real footage, no AI, no over-polish) while fixing proof density and openers.

## 5. Rubric T1-T8 (condensed working copy; gates T1, T2, T5; a gate at 2 or lower blocks presentation)
| # | Criterion | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 gate | hook opens on the result (0-3 s) | first sentence is a number or an extreme moment, proof by 2 s | a strong claim without a number | name and age, "hey", or a logo opening |
| T2 gate | proof density | every spoken number appears on screen; speech-to-proof about 1:1; a visual change every <= 3 s | some numbers proven | no proof, or the number only in a title |
| T3 | soundbite choice | number soundbite in the hook and again at the result; emotional soundbite closes uncut | strong soundbite only mid-video | strong soundbite missing or the cut changes meaning |
| T4 | arc before-turn-after | pain quantified, the product enters after the problem, a tangible after | list of benefits, no before | monologue of praise |
| T5 gate | credibility and compliance | real customer in raw footage, lower-third, consent exists, cuts keep meaning, polish does not erase authenticity | no lower-third, viewer does not know who speaks | AI customer/voice/result, or sentences stitched to change meaning (disqualifying) |
| T6 | readable without sound | captions with a keyword colour, a result headline, number callouts; story clear muted | template captions only | no captions or broken RTL |
| T7 | length and dead time | target length, no long thank-yous or silence | 30-50 % over target | twice the target or more |
| T8 | CTA and ending | emotional soundbite, then the customer's CTA and an end card <= 3 s | verbal CTA only, no card | silence or black over 5 s, ending mid-sentence |
Critic notes: a timecode for every score under 4 and a concrete fix ("move 59.8-63.8 to 0:00 and add the screenshot with a count-up chip"). Keep the signature (fixed word-pop captions, split-screen proof) and add the upgrade; where the signature conflicts with the market, say so. Rubric thresholds (average >= 4.0, no dimension < 3) are the owner's gate, historical, not research-proven.

## 6. Workflow additions specific to the type
- Asset checklist to request from the client: the original selfie (not via a chat app), vertical, 30 fps, facing a window, quiet, not driving; result screenshots and before/after photos; life B-roll; logo and brand colours; name, age, role, time in the programme; the SIGNED consent including ad use.
- Moodboard references: pick 3-4 testimonial references with different jobs (incident-first hook with 1:1 proof, three numbers in three seconds with a persistent headline, a vertical black-and-white before with brand-coloured keywords, live proof by 4 s); do not reuse their material.
- Variants from every interview via `multi-video-variants`: hook A/B/C on one body, "no music" and "no captions" versions.
